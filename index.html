COVER LETTER

Hello,

I'm Dex Cimino, a multidisciplinary 3D artist and creative technologist who works comfortably across visual production, game development, and AI-assisted building. I began my education in engineering before shifting toward the arts, a path that continues to shape how I approach creative and technical work. I do my best work where strong creative judgment, technical curiosity, and the ability to turn an idea into something real all matter.

My professional background spans 3D characters, props, creatures, environments, texturing, rigging, animation, optimization, and graphic design. I have created production assets for Roblox adaptations of major film properties, supported independent game and Minecraft projects, and led art production across a team of eight artists. That range taught me how to adapt quickly, communicate clearly, and deliver within real technical and visual constraints.

I have also pushed beyond a traditional art role. I integrated AI into Roblox Studio production work and independently built DexNote and NodeBlast using Claude Code. I use AI as a practical extension of the creative process: to prototype faster, explore options, solve unfamiliar problems, automate repetitive work, and move projects toward a finished result.

What I bring is a broad but connected skill set: visual judgment, hands-on production experience, independent ownership, and a habit of learning whatever the work requires. Whether the immediate need is creating assets, evaluating visual output, improving a workflow, prototyping a tool, or helping a team solve an unusual problem, I can contribute quickly and keep moving without losing sight of quality.

I would welcome the opportunity to bring that mix of creativity, adaptability, and execution to your team. Thank you for your time and consideration.
