# Asset credits

Every file in `assets/` gets a row here, with its source and licence.
**No row, no ship.** Carried over with the T1 transplant from the lookdev
testbed, whose rule this is.

| path | source | author | licence | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `luts/identity.3dl` | generated by `tools/bake_lut.py` | this project | CC0 | 33³ neutral grading LUT. Every world ships this at T1 — see `NEUTRAL_LUT` in `js/tune.js` |
| `textures/flat.webp` | `tools/bake_terrain_maps.py` from `marble_cliff_06` | Amal Kumar / Poly Haven | CC0 | 512² packed triplanar map: normal XY in RG, albedo luminance in B |
| `textures/steep.webp` | `tools/bake_terrain_maps.py` from `marble_cliff_03` | Amal Kumar / Poly Haven | CC0 | as above. The cliff-face layer, tiled tightest |
| `textures/high.webp` | `tools/bake_terrain_maps.py` from `marble_cliff_04` | Amal Kumar / Poly Haven | CC0 | as above. The high-ground layer |

The three terrain maps are derivatives of the lookdev testbed's scan family —
one collection, one capture, all six members tagged stratified/striated, which
is the rock character this look asks for. The `_alb.jpg` / `_nrm.png` sources
live there and not here; `tools/bake_terrain_maps.py --source <lookdev>/assets/textures`
reproduces all three.

## What the LUT baker also writes, and why it is not here

`tools/bake_lut.py` emits three LUTs. Only the one above is committed, because
only the one above is loaded:

- `home-golden-hour.3dl` — the lookdev testbed's active grade. It is the A/B
  control that proves the plumbing is not neutral by accident, and it was used
  for exactly that during T1. Nothing loads it at runtime.
- `night-bioluminescent.3dl` — the archived dark grade, kept as the lighting
  reference for Ember and Shroud.

Both are half a megabyte of text apiece on a page that already carries an 8 MB
Babylon build, and `python tools/bake_lut.py` reproduces all three byte for byte
in under a second. They are gitignored at the repo root rather than shipped, and
the moment a world's `lut` points at one, it gets committed and a row here.
