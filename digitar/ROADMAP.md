# DIGITAR ROADMAP (v1, 2026-10-15)

**End state (Dex's vision):** a digital version of a person good enough to work as them — messages, applications, descriptions, promotions, gameplay/streaming presence — with voice mapped onto avatars he designs (e.g., his own 3D models/characters), head-tracked and lip-synced like avatar streamers (the Burnt Peanut pattern), and a repeatable system other people can follow to digitize themselves.

## Phase 1 — TEXT BRAIN (now)
- Archive structure live (this folder); raw captures appended after every significant voice dump; profile distilled continuously.
- Instruments: the GPT questionnaire (methods/), his-edit captures (every draft Dex edits by hand is training data — file the diffs), claims ledger discipline on ALL outbound copy.
- Rules: text-first, lean, dated, supersede-don't-overwrite, register-tagged captures.
- Later instruments (queued ideas): a "refusals & red lines" session; a "describe my products in my words" session per app (DexNote/YapTap/YouVerse/Inko/MindSplit/Möbius); a money-and-terms honesty session; a clean 10-minute voice sample read (for Phase 2).

## Phase 2 — VOICE
- Capture a deliberate clean voice corpus (quiet room, consistent mic, scripted + freeform) — today's dictations are content-rich but acoustically messy.
- Evaluate voice-clone options when Dex green-lights; clone used only for HIS content, with per-use consent noted in the archive.

## Phase 3 — AVATAR PERFORMANCE
- Dex models/rigs his own avatar(s) (his 3D craft is the moat — nobody else's avatar looks like his work).
- Head/face tracking via webcam (MediaPipe-class tooling) → avatar follows his head, mouth syncs to his voice (live or cloned), arms/gesture as tooling allows.
- Uses: streaming/gameplay presence, video pieces, product demos where "Dex" presents.

## Phase 4 — THE KIT (for others)
- The system IS the product: capture protocol + folder schema + claims discipline + register dial, packaged so anyone can build their own digitar. Dex's archive becomes the reference implementation.
- Open questions to design later: consent & licensing of a person's digitar; what happens to it if they're gone; impersonation safeguards (as the model improves, access control matters MORE, not less).

## Standing risks noted at inception
- Fluent-false drift (the founding incident) — mitigated by the claims ledger + pre-send audit.
- Archive rot: people change; entries carry dates; a yearly re-interview refreshes the top layer.
- Exposure: this repo copy is Git-backed per Dex's call and excluded from the web deploy; if collaborators ever join the repo, re-gate the raw layer.
