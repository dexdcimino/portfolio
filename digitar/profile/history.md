# HISTORY — the timeline, as he tells it (v2, 2026-10-15; enriched by GPT interview Q1 (both parts))

- **Childhood:** creative from the start — clay & molding, painting, colors, drawing, sculpting, wild imagination, his own creative world. At home, art was framed as a hobby; engineering was the safe path (older brother — 10 years his senior — an engineer he followed; his pops a welder: conservative, old-school, loving). He was good with numbers & logic but pulled to art: "there is no option — I have to be doing creative work."
- **School:** Red Rocks Community College on the math/engineering track, set up to transfer to the **Colorado School of Mines** — he never transferred; before the move it hit him ("shit, this is not what I want to be doing for a living") and he switched. In & out of college ~5–6 years across many focuses (traditional arts, logo design, programming/web design — a bad Python class teaching tax programs soured him on programming at the time). **Only completed degree: Associate of Arts, Red Rocks (2013)**; he was also close to an AS in Math & Science (not completed), no bachelor's. The core difficulty: he loved too many creative pursuits to pick one for 30–50 years, so he left to figure it out as he went.
- **First paid design:** his biker parents' circle — poker-chip art for biker bars, t-shirt logos (~$100 gigs); MicroBurst logo competitions (won ~2). **Quality Lapel Pins — first real job** (pins, lanyards, keychains; designed in Illustrator): "a fantastic job" that he couldn't stand — desk repetition is a genuine intolerable for him; he left.
- **Ages ~23–26:** personal chapter (raw only — see raw capture Q1 part 2; never for outreach). He course-corrected hard from it: discipline, fitness, work focus.
- **FASTSIGNS & other design work** sit in this span per his resume.
- **Black & Red Inc. (late 20s):** advertising company based at a farmhouse near Boulder; built ads in **Hype** software. There he picked up **ZBrush & Maya** — "couldn't put it down" — and the ~7-year 3D pursuit began. ~1 year there; COVID pushed them remote; he left to build a 3D portfolio.
- **California stretch:** moved out with his cousin, came back, drove out again; slept in his car for a while building the portfolio — then landed Pyxelchain.
- **Pyxelchain Technology — 3D Art Lead, 2021–2023:** first 3D artist hired → lead; team of 8 artists (later 6) in a ~70-person company riding the NFT boom; the product was an NFT kitten game (not his taste; the job — the tools, remote work, leading — he loved). His brother joined as VP of Engineering. The CEO invited him to live in Texas for ~a year. Company went out of business with the bust.
- **Knights of Edengale — 3D Generalist, 2023–2024. Super League — 3D Artist, 2024–2025** (Roblox tie-ins: Despicable Me 4, The Wild Robot, Sonic 3, Dog Man, Transformers One). **Embody Games — 3D Artist, 2025–present** (does not embrace AI — part of why he's looking).
- **Since ~30 (he is 34):** no consistent income — gigs of a few grand here & there — alongside heavy skill growth: Maya, ZBrush, Substance Painter, **3D Coat**, full Adobe Suite (**Animate**, Illustrator, Photoshop pro-level).
- **Side games across the years:** with his brother (an engineer) and with **David Strackhan** (a London dev he reached out to): Total Smashout and **Undercrude ("Under Crude") — on Steam**, a tiny but real shipped credit. His deepest professional pain: thousands of hours across the tools without ever landing a shipped TITLE / AAA credit — "brutal." Flow state is his deepest work mode, found in games work end-to-end (2D art, animation, design, interface).
- **The AI turn (Q1, his account):** came in slowly, then exploded. First real use: building his FIRST portfolio website (GPT-3/4 era, possibly lost now) with a little game on it. He grew WITH the models, pushing each capability jump; the last ~6 months are the eruption. In roughly two months of very long days he built the current dexcimino.com portfolio of working apps (DexNote, YapTap, YouVerse, Inko, MindSplit, Möbius).
- **2026-10:** full-scale search for an AI-first team/home with Bash + GPT as his agents; Digitar project started 2026-10-15.
