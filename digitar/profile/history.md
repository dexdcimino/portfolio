# HISTORY — the timeline, as he tells it (v1, 2026-10-15)

- **School:** started in math & engineering — expected to be an engineer his whole life; artist at heart; switched his degree midway into 2D/3D modeling, concepting, logo design, animation. Associate's (AA), Red Rocks Community College, 2013.
- **The long stretch (~15 years pursuing the arts since the switch):** "a struggle — very little coin, and a couple awesome opportunities along the way." Jobs in the creative field, mostly sparse. Confidence + not putting himself out there (social media/YouTube) cost him — his own assessment.
- **Early design work:** Quality Lapel Pins, BittyPets (2D), Black & Red Inc., FASTSIGNS (graphic/product design).
- **Games (indie since ~2012) & the professional 3D years:**
  - Pyxelchain Technology — 3D Art Lead, 2021–2023 (led a team of 8)
  - Knights of Edengale — 3D Generalist, 2023–2024
  - Super League — 3D Artist, 2024–2025 (Roblox adaptations/tie-ins: Despicable Me 4, The Wild Robot, Sonic 3, Dog Man, Transformers One)
  - Embody Games — 3D Artist, 2025–present (current; does not embrace AI — part of why he's looking)
- **The AI turn (his account, GPT interview Q1, 2026-10-15):** came in slowly, "like it did for everybody," then exploded exponentially. First real use: building his FIRST portfolio website (GPT-3/4 era) — an even earlier portfolio than the "old portfolio" featured on his current site, possibly lost now — with a little game on it. He grew WITH the models, pushing each new capability, constantly replacing & building on his own work; the last ~6 months are the eruption ("now anybody can build anything on a computer with just a little bit of discipline and the right idea"). Began directing AI agents to build software. In roughly two months of very long days he built the whole dexcimino.com portfolio of working apps (DexNote, YapTap, YouVerse, Inko, MindSplit, Möbius) — art foundation + AI direction = his differentiator.
- **2026-10:** running a full-scale search for an AI-first team/home (startups, contract studios, applications) with Bash + GPT as his agents; Digitar project started 2026-10-15.
