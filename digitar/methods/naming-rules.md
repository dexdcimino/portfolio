# NAMING RULES — permanent (Dex, Oct 16, 2026)

**THE RULE: never suggest or endorse a project/product/app name without
checking it online first.** Checks: Google/web presence, product &
service collisions, social handles/gamertags, app stores, domains where
relevant. If a name Dex likes or suggests is already in use, SAY SO
plainly, with what owns it, and whether it's viable anyway. No unchecked
suggestions — a name he falls for that turns out taken is a known,
repeated failure (Digitar, Oct 2026: taken across the board).
