Inko privacy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Your drawings

Signed out, drawings are kept only on your own device. Signed in, the drawings you save are also kept with your account, so the same gallery is on every device you sign in on. Only you can see them unless you make a drawing public. The first time you sign in on a device, the drawings you made there signed out move into your account.

Signing in

An account is only needed to keep your drawings on more than one device, to share drawings, and to react to them. You can sign in with Google, with Discord, or with a name and password.

  • Google: we receive your Google account ID, name and email address. We keep only the account ID, to recognise you next time. Your email is used once, at sign-in, and is not stored.
  • Discord: we receive your Discord user ID and username. We keep only the user ID.
  • Name and password: we keep your name and a salted hash of your password, never the password itself.

What is public

Your gallery name (@name), your profile picture if you choose one (a square cut from one of your drawings, which updates as that drawing changes), the drawings you choose to make public, and reaction counts are visible to everyone. Anyone can search for a gallery name. Which reaction you gave a drawing is stored with your account so you can change it.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing your data with anyone. Data is stored with our hosting provider, Vercel, only to run the app and the gallery.

Deleting

Make a drawing private and its public copy is removed. Delete a drawing while signed in and it is removed from your account on every device. Delete your account from the account sheet in the app and your account, your saved and public drawings and your Google or Discord link are all removed from the server; the drawings on the device you deleted it from stay there.
