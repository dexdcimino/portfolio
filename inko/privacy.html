Inko privacy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Your drawings

Drawings are kept on your own device. Nothing you draw leaves it unless you make that drawing public.

Signing in

An account is only needed to share drawings and react to them. You can sign in with Google, with Discord, or with a name and password.

  • Google: we receive your Google account ID, name and email address. We keep only the account ID, to recognise you next time. Your email is used once, at sign-in, and is not stored.
  • Discord: we receive your Discord user ID and username. We keep only the user ID.
  • Name and password: we keep your name and a salted hash of your password, never the password itself.

What is public

Your gallery name (@name), the drawings you choose to make public, and reaction counts are visible to everyone. Which reaction you gave a drawing is stored with your account so you can change it.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing your data with anyone. Data is stored with our hosting provider, Vercel, only to run the gallery.

Deleting

Make a drawing private and it is removed from the server. Delete your account from the account sheet in the app and your account, your public drawings and your Google or Discord link are all removed.
