# Brain Dump

> PLACEHOLDER — replace with the real Brain Dump prompt.

Take everything below the line and turn it into something I can act on. It is
unsorted, half-finished, and written in the order it occurred to me. Do not ask
me to organise it first — that is the job.

## What to do with it

1. **Sort it.** Group related fragments together, even when I wrote them pages
   apart. Name each group in my words, not yours.
2. **Separate the kinds.** A task, an idea, a worry and a fact are four different
   things and need four different treatments. Label which is which.
3. **Find the real item.** Where a fragment is a symptom, say what the underlying
   thing is and mark it clearly as your inference, not mine.
4. **Rank by cost of delay**, not by effort. What gets worse if it waits a month?
5. **Say what is missing.** Anything I clearly meant to write down and did not.

## Rules

- Do not invent detail I did not give you. If something is ambiguous, list it as
  a question instead of guessing.
- Keep my phrasing where it is specific. Rewrite it only where it is vague.
- No preamble, no summary of what you are about to do, no closing pep talk.
- If a fragment is genuinely unusable, say so and drop it. Do not pad.

## Output

- **Now** — this week, in order.
- **Next** — real, but not yet.
- **Open questions** — things only I can answer.
- **Dropped** — with one line each on why.
